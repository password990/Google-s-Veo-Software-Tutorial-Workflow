## Locked facts

- Working group meeting: 12 September 2026, 18:30–19:35 at Riverside Hall.
- Proposed project: four raised beds; two to be reachable from an accessible path. This is a planning concept, not an approved design.
- Survey: 86 informal, self-selected responses collected 1–8 September 2026. 59 support exploring a pilot (68.6%); 18 unsure (20.9%); 9 do not support (10.5%). These are directional, not representative.
- Multiple selections: fresh produce 62; meeting neighbours 55; youth learning 44; upkeep concern 37; water concern 31; accessible-path concern 18.
- Preliminary one-time setup estimate: $6,850 before tax. Annual operations estimate: $1,750. A 10% setup reserve is $685, yielding $7,535 before tax.
- All prices are illustrative USD planning values. No supplier quotes were obtained. Tax, permits, major drainage, insurance changes, and volunteer labour are excluded.
- Volunteer condition: 8 recurring volunteers plus one backup contact.
- Decision status: feasibility phase may be proposed; no construction or spending authorization has been granted. Site confirmation, accessible layout, volunteer rota, and written prices remain open.

## Source-to-output map

- Survey support counts and themes → final brief “Why consider a pilot”; retain method limitation.
- Meeting status, owners, conditions → final brief “Conditions to resolve first” and “Decision requested.”
- Budget totals and exclusions → final brief “Planning cost range”; call them estimates, not quotes.
- Final recommendation → authorize quote gathering and feasibility work only; no spending or building.
