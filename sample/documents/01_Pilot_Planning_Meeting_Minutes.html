Discussion and decisions

Proposed site and scope

The group reviewed the unused paved edge of the Riverside Hall lot as a possible site. Facilities liaison Sofia Green will confirm ownership, access conditions, drainage, and whether a written site agreement is required. The group agreed to plan around four raised beds, including two beds reachable from an accessible path. This is a planning assumption, not an approved site plan.

Community input

Priya Shah summarized a short neighbourhood pulse survey completed by 86 respondents. Fifty-nine respondents selected “support”; the group noted that the survey was informal and self-selected. Members asked that the proposal describe the results as an early signal rather than a representative measure of neighbourhood opinion.

Operations and maintenance

Leon Okafor proposed a rotating weekly watering and inspection schedule. Members agreed that the pilot should not begin until at least eight volunteers have confirmed recurring shifts and one backup contact has been named. Ruth Campbell will draft a one-page rota and identify a safe tool-storage option.

Budget approach

The group reviewed a preliminary planning estimate of $6,850 for setup and $1,750 for annual operating costs. These figures are order-of-magnitude estimates, exclude taxes and volunteer labour, and require written supplier quotes before any funding request. No funds were committed.
