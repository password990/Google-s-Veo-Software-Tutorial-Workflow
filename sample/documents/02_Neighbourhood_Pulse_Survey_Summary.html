Implications for planning

  • Treat volunteer capacity and maintenance as design requirements, not details to solve later.
  • Show how an accessible route would reach at least part of the growing area before asking for approval.
  • Keep the next consultation open to residents who were unsure or opposed; this survey does not explain their reasons.

Suggested wording for a decision brief

SAFE SUMMARY In an informal survey of 86 respondents, 59 (68.6%) supported exploring a garden pilot. Respondents also raised maintenance, water access, and path accessibility as planning considerations. The results are directional and not representative of all residents.
